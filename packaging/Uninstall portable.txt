Transcription Flow (portable): how to remove it
================================================

1. Quit Transcription Flow: right-click its tray icon, Quit.
2. If you turned on "Start with Windows" in Settings, turn it off there
   first (or it stays in Task Manager > Startup apps).
3. Delete this TranscriptionFlow folder.
4. To remove your settings and downloaded speech models too, delete:
     %LOCALAPPDATA%\TranscriptionFlow   (models and logs)
     %APPDATA%\TranscriptionFlow        (settings)
   Paste each line into the File Explorer address bar to open it.
